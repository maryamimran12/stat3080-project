## Directions
To view the slides on your local machine, you must

1. Download the archive, e.g. `Lec1_STAT3080_S26_files.zip`.
2. Decompress the contents into a convenient location on your local machine.
3. Double-click the html file.
4. In Chrome, click anywhere on a slide and click `F` or `Esc` to toggle between full screen and not full screen.
5. Hit `Esc` to see the slide previews.
6. Hit the blue burger icon in the bottom left to see the presentation outline.
7. Use the arrow keys to advance or rewind the slides.
